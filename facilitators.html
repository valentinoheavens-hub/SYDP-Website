Meet Our Facilitators

The Faculty Behind
the SYDP Curriculum

Eight practitioners — spanning venture building, law, technology, leadership and academia — deliver the SYDP 2026 11-module Business Excellence Framework.

Ms. Perpetual-Love Uduma NBDSP

Principal Consultant, Auxilium Max Company LTD — Business Venture Studio

Facilitates Module 1 (Mindset Transformation & Personal Effectiveness), Module 5 (Enterprise Discovery & Opportunity Identification) and co-facilitates Module 11 (Business Packaging, Pitch Development & Investor Readiness).

Dr. Valentino Heavens

Founder & CEO, BlackBelt Global Consulting Limited & BlackBeltCEO Venture Studio

Event Host and facilitator of Module 2 (Smart Digital Technology), Module 7 (Business Plan & Business Model Development), Module 9 (Financial Literacy & Wealth Creation), Module 10 (Marketing, Branding & Customer Acquisition) and co-facilitates Module 11.

Emine Yilmaz Bütün MSc. CPA

International Strategy & Investment Advisor · Chair, ITT Nexus

Facilitates Module 6 (Market Research, Financial Projection & Valuation) and delivers a special presentation on International Strategy & Investment at the Opening Ceremony.

Mr. Oluwashina Oliyide

Chief Executive Officer, Gnovia Limited

Delivers Artificial Intelligence for Management Excellence, equipping participants with practical AI tools for business operations.

Rebecca Lilley LLB, MSc

Chartered Legal Executive · AML Auditor · Founder, Poise2Passage

Brings legal, compliance and anti-money-laundering expertise to the SYDP faculty, supporting participants' governance and regulatory readiness.

Dr. Nath Aghraghra

Executive Principal, PALMS Africa

Facilitates Module 3 (Leadership Excellence for Entrepreneurs & Managers) and leads the alternative incubation track for participants outside the top pitch cohort.

Dr. (Arc.) David Adio-Moses

Senior Lecturer, University of Lagos

UNILAG Liaison who facilitates Module 8 (Project Management Fundamentals), the TEND Framework session, and Project Management Principles at the Opening Ceremony.

Amb. Victor Adetolaju MITPN

Founder, Association for Students' Social Welfare · Chairman, Students Chamber of Commerce

Champions youth empowerment and student engagement across the SYDP cohort as an ecosystem partner and facilitator.

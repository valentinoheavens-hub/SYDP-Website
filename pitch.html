Your Submission Should Include

  • Business Name
  • Team Members
  • Business Plan
  • Business Model
  • Vision Statement
  • Value Proposition
  • Marketing Strategy
  • Cash Flow Projection
  • Any Supporting Information
📊

Pitch Deck Guidelines 10–12 slides

  1. Problem & Opportunity
  2. Your Solution
  3. Market Opportunity (TAM, SAM & SOM)
  4. Business Model
  5. Competitive Advantage
  6. Traction & Milestones
  7. Marketing & Sales Strategy
  8. Financial Projections
  9. Founding Team
  10. Funding Ask & Use of Funds
  11. Exit Strategy (optional)

Create a compelling story, support it with data, and clearly demonstrate why your business is investment-ready.
